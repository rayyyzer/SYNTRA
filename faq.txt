About this FAQ. 
This document consolidates questions raised during the introduction session with the official launch material and organizer clarifications. Where detailed test cases are involved, the FAQ reflects the announced release date of 16 September.
Team Formation & Eligibility
Q1. What is the minimum team size?
A. 
The minimum team size is 1 member. Individual participation is allowed.
Q2. What is the maximum team size?
A. 
A team can have a maximum of 4 members.
Q3. Can I participate individually?
A. 
Yes. A single student can participate as a team of one.
Q4. Do all team members need to be from the same college?
A. 
Yes. All members of a team must belong to the same college.
Q5. Are there any restrictions on branch or degree?
A. 
No. There are no restrictions on branch or degree. Students from different branches may form a team, and UG/PG students are eligible.
Q6. Can one team attempt more than one theme/problem statement?
A. 
No. One team can participate in one theme only.
Q7. What if students want to participate in multiple themes?
A. A separate team must be formed and registered for each theme. The same members may form a separate team for another theme, but the primary member (team leader) must be different. Each registered team is associated with only one theme.

Team registration closes on 16 September 2026 at 11:59 PM.

11. How does caching work and why is it important?
The scorer sends the same query twice — first call is "cold" (no cache), second call should be a "cache hit" (fast, ≤300ms p95). Paraphrases of the original query should also hit the cache (semantic matching). This tests whether your system recognizes that different phrasings map to the same troubleshooting scenario.
Implementation tip: Cache by normalized query intent, not exact string match. Use embedding similarity or keyword overlap to match paraphrases to cached entries.
12. What are "unseen scenarios" in the generalization probe?
Judges have hidden test queries not in the 20-scenario student kit. These are sent with a new

ContextDeeplinkResponse
 for these unseen scenarios. This tests whether your system generalizes beyond memorized examples.

14. Can I invent steps that aren't in the SIIS text?
No. The SIIS response is your source of truth. You must derive all actions and steps from the text provided. Inventing steps not present in the SIIS content will be penalized. You can rephrase and restructure, but the semantic content must come from the SIIS text.

21. What happens during the live demo?
Judges will:
Call your /health endpoint to confirm the API is up
Send canonical queries from the kit and measure cold-start latency
Repeat the same query to test cache hit and latency
Send paraphrases to test semantic cache matching
Send unseen scenarios with new SIIS payloads to test generalization
Your API must be running and accessible during the evaluation window.
